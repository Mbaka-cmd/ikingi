Overview

This is an information website for {{ school.name }}. It has no user accounts, no student or parent portal and no online registration.

Information this website collects

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. We do not collect personal information automatically.

Enquiries

If an enquiry form is shown on the Contact page, the details you type are used only to prepare a message that opens in WhatsApp or your email app on your own device. The message is sent only if you press Send in that app. Nothing you type in the form is stored on this website.

Please do not send sensitive student information, such as birth certificates, identity documents or medical records, through this website.

Hosting

Like most websites, this site is delivered by a hosting provider, which may keep standard technical logs (for example, IP address and pages requested) for security and operation.

Links to other services

This website may link to services such as WhatsApp or Google Maps. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact the school.

Accessibility

We aim to make this website easy to use for everyone, including people who use a keyboard or a screen reader. If you have difficulty using any part of this website, please contact the school and we will help you with the information you need.
